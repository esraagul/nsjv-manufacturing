North San Joaquin Valley — Manufacturing Industry, Skills & Careers

A look at the region's manufacturing sector: how big it is, which sub-industries drive it, the occupations that do the work, the skills they need, and how those skills transfer between jobs. Covers the Stockton-Lodi, Modesto & Merced metro areas (San Joaquin, Stanislaus & Merced counties). All figures from official O*NET and California EDD data.

Skills most in demand

Skills come from O*NET, which rates how important each skill is for every occupation on a scale of 1 to 5. Each bar is that rating averaged across the 16 occupations, with larger occupations counting more (weighted by how many people they employ). A longer bar means the skill matters across more of the manufacturing workforce. All 35 skills.

Knowledge most in demand

Knowledge areas are the subjects a job requires expertise in, such as production and processing, mechanical and mathematics. O*NET rates each one from 1 to 5 for every occupation. Each bar is that rating averaged across the 16 occupations, with larger occupations counting more (weighted by how many people they employ). This is where the manufacturing-specific content shows up. Top 15 of 33 areas.
